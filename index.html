      QUANTUM RADAR / ATOMIC MODELS

      Compare the legacy fixed-orbit model with the quantum mechanical probability model.

      LEGACY NAVIGATION MODEL BOHR MODEL
      SHELL 1: 2 e⁻ · SHELL 2: 6 e⁻

      Bohr proposed that electrons occupy specific quantized energy levels around the nucleus. The model explained key features of hydrogen's emission spectrum, but it does not fully describe multi-electron atoms.

      QUANTUM RADAR SCHRÖDINGER MODEL

      The quantum mechanical model describes electrons using wavefunctions. Orbitals represent probability distributions for where electrons may be found. Unlike the Bohr model, electrons are not assigned exact circular paths.

      WHERE THE AIRPORT ANALOGY ENDS

      • Electrons are not tiny people.
      • They do not wait at literal gates.
      • Orbitals are not physical rooms.
      • Electrons do not travel along visible airport routes.
      • Quantum transitions are changes between allowed energy states, not physical flights.
      • The airport system is only a visual model for organization and energy behavior.
